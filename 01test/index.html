Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the safe, responsible, and respectful use of technology. It includes the rules and the standards for human behavior on the internet. If you use the internet to communicate, work, or learn, you are a digital citizen.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law is the legal rights and restrictions governing technology use. It includes the rules and regulations for using technology, including copyright laws, privacy laws, and online safety laws.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules and guidelines that outline the acceptable use of technology and the internet. It is designed to protect users and ensure that technology is used in a safe and responsible manner.

What Is Plagerism

Plagiarism is the act of using the work of another person without giving credit. When you do this, you falsely state that the work is your own.

What are the rules for ai generated content

Web designers use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to work faster. But, they must obey specific rules to maintain website quality and security.Do not keep default settings: AI tools frequently make generic designs with the same fonts and layouts. Designers must change these settings to make a unique brand identity. Do a human review: You can use AI to write text, create code, or make metadata. But, a human must examine this work before it goes on the internet. Do not use AI accessibility overlays. These tools can cause errors for screen readers. Protect user data: Do not put private user data into public AI systems. If you install an AI chatbot on a website, you must verify that the chatbot does not share data with other organizations.
